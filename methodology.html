Observatory Methodology

REFERENCE DEMONSTRATION

One real save, a bounded semantic catalogue, and explicit model limits

This Observatory is a presentation-safe reconstruction of one completed Witcher 3 playthrough. The save is imported as host evidence, mapped into semantic Historical Reality, compiled into Current State, and then evaluated by two separate analytical systems: probable-future pressure and information propagation.

The demo does not claim to model every quest, NPC, contract, encounter, or rumor in The Witcher 3. When the catalogue or propagation policy does not contain an authored interpretation, Living World leaves it unmodelled rather than inventing one.

AUTHORITY

What each layer is allowed to say

Host ObservationEvidence imported from the save. It is not itself narrative interpretation.
Historical RealitySemantic events the evidence establishes as having happened in this playthrough.
Current StateWhat remains authoritatively true NOW after supersession and resolution are applied.
Analytical surfacesProbable futures and character knowledge. They can change without creating history.

The critical boundary is simple: probability, pressure, knowledge, and transmission never create Historical Reality. Historical events require authoritative evidence or an explicit future realization mechanism. The current public demo has no autonomous realization mechanism.

The last reconstructed historical event in this reference playthrough occurs on WD75. The save-derived NOW is WD87. That gap is intentional: World Day can advance without requiring a new historical event every day.

PROBABLE FUTURE

Pressure is a deterministic model score, not a prediction percentage

The baseline future engine is deliberately small and explainable. Every historical event can emit semantic causal seeds. A seed's authored salience, detail-decay profile, persistence, and explicit semantic resolvers determine its lifecycle. The engine does not use machine-learned probabilities and does not claim statistical likelihood.

InputBaseline behavior
SaliencePeak pressure: low 0.25, medium 0.50, high 0.75, very high 1.00.
Detail decayRise to peak: fast 2 days, medium 5, slow 10. Base decay window: fast 14 days, medium 35, slow 70.
PersistenceTimed decay is adjusted by persistence. Medium uses 0.75×, durable 1.5×, quest-chain 1.0×. Permanent / until-changed context does not receive a fabricated expiry.
Explicit resolverIf a later semantic historical event resolves or supersedes the seed, the future becomes RESOLVED.
Conditional persistence without a resolverThe baseline returns INDETERMINATE rather than inventing a timed outcome.

Only ACTIVE probable futures are published to the public Frontier. Persistent context remains part of reconstruction analysis but is not presented as an unresolved opportunity.

INFORMATION PROPAGATION

What happened and who knows about it are separate questions

Historical Reality determines what information can exist. A separate authored propagation policy determines where a semantic fact begins, which holders and channels are eligible, how confidence and distortion change, and which transmissions succeed as World Day advances.

Historical eventAn event in this save emits semantic causal seeds.
Propagation bindingAn authored policy declares origin holders, channels, routes, and transmission behavior.
Knowledge stateSuccessful routes create holder occurrences with acquisition day, confidence, channel, and distortion.
Conversation cueA later system can ask whether a specific character actually knows enough for dialogue, correspondence, or another realization.

The original authored propagation domain contains 95 propagatable bindings and 3 state-only bindings. The reconstructed Blood and Wine catalogue currently contributes 59 additional seeds without authored propagation bindings. Those seeds remain unbound; Living World does not invent origin holders or routes just to make the map busier.

At WD87 the reference save contains 437 accumulated information-holder occurrences. The exact WD87 next-day transmission Frontier is empty, which simply means no authored route is due on the following day. Across WD0–WD87, 55 snapshots expose at least one information-transmission candidate.

Regional Spread visualizes accumulated knowledge or genuine information-transmission candidates. It does not convert probable-future pressure into geographic spread. Transmission probability belongs only to the route model.

Why two similar playthroughs may share rumors

The information model is deterministic. If two saves establish the same semantic history and use the same authored propagation policy, much of their knowledge state should be the same. That is expected. Divergence comes from different historical outcomes, surviving actors, relationships, secrets, and which causal seeds exist in each playthrough.

PUBLICATION BOUNDARY

What the public Observatory deliberately does not expose

The browser receives presentation-safe semantic snapshots. It does not receive the raw save or the private evidence used to reconstruct it.

  • No raw .sav data.
  • No native FactsDB identifiers, native game-time values, record offsets, entry values, or query sums.
  • No Host Observation IDs, save hashes, private evidence objects, or private provenance.
  • No GM-only state or master-database internals.

This separation allows the Observatory to explain causality without turning the public site into an evidence dump.

NEXT COVERAGE

What changes as more authored data is added

The next major enrichment is the Wild Hunt optional-quest catalogue. The save does not need to change. Adding defensible semantic coverage should allow the same imported playthrough to reconstruct more Historical Reality, more Current State, more causal seeds, more probable-future opportunities, and more information capable of propagating.

A later architectural cleanup will separate reusable propagation-catalogue data even more explicitly from per-save Historical Reality. That refactor should preserve the observable behavior shown here rather than redefine it.
